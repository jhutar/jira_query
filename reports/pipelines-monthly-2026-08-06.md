# Team Pipelines range monthly
* **Overview:** The team closed 7 tickets this period, headlined by a CI cron-schedule redesign that cut peak concurrent test-cluster usage from ~12-15 to ~4-5, delivery of 9 live Grafana dashboards for Results/Pipelines/Chains, and completed OpenTelemetry metric-validation work for the OpenCensus migration. In review, a new PostgreSQL-backed report-generation pipeline for CPT performance summaries produced its first draft KB articles.
* **What was done:**
  * Redesigned the performance CI cron schedule ([SRVKP-12781](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-12781), 2sp), cutting normal-day concurrency from ~12-15 to ~4 and keeping all scenarios within the Boskos 10-lease limit; also fixed a Blocker-severity bug in `horreum_api.py` where label creation failed with HTTP 400 ([SRVKP-12591](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-12591), 1sp).
  * Shipped Horreum/Grafana observability work: configured Horreum schemas and Grafana dashboards for Results ingestion/API metrics, adding 5 new dashboards and refactoring 4 existing ones onto shared libraries — all 9 now live ([SRVKP-12483](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-12483), 5sp, PRs [#112](https://github.com/openshift-pipelines/performance/pull/112) and [#115](https://github.com/openshift-pipelines/performance/pull/115)) — and fixed broken change-detection graphs in the Horreum UI ([SRVKP-12534](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-12534), 2sp).
  * Completed Critical-priority OpenTelemetry metric validation across Pipelines, Chains, and Results controllers for the OpenCensus-to-OpenTelemetry migration ([SRVKP-12643](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-12643), 3sp), and closed out scenario-planning groundwork by spinning off follow-up epics under the CPT feature ([SRVKP-7566](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-7566), 2sp).
* **What is moving:**
  * CPT report-generation tooling is in review: a Critical-priority PostgreSQL-driven pipeline with LLM prompt templates now produces first-draft customer-facing KB/benchmark reports ([SRVKP-11997](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-11997), 4sp), and a feasibility study concluded direct PostgreSQL querying is preferable to Grafana MCP to avoid coupling reports to dashboard layout ([SRVKP-12533](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-12533), 3sp).
  * Tooling to manage perf cron jobs in `openshift/release` is in review but blocked: the MR ([openshift/release#82005](https://github.com/openshift/release/pull/82005)) fails its `check-gh-automation` check because the merge bot lacks admin access on the target repo ([SRVKP-12000](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-12000), 2sp).
  * Several in-progress items show no updates this period and may need a status check: resolver duration-metric reliability ([SRVKP-13220](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-13220)), resolver regression testing for Pipelines 1.22 vs 1.23 ([SRVKP-13158](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-13158)), the PipelineRun count/metrics discrepancy investigation ([SRVKP-9637](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-9637), Critical), and the CPT phase 1/2 epics ([SRVKP-7149](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-7149), [SRVKP-10874](https://redhat.atlassian.net/browse/SRVKP-10874)).
